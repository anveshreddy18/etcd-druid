Title: The solution you didn't know existed: Operationalizing etcd clusters at Scale

Abstract:

Imagine running highly available, distributed kv store like etcd across tens of thousands of production Kubernetes clusters managing highly critical and sensitive data. The stakes are high, and the consequences of failure are severe, think data corruption, quorum loss, member failures, and storage bloat. These issues can disrupt clusters and are complex to manage manually, let alone all the additional functionality you need to implement, making them error-prone and difficult to scale and harder to operate.

What if there was a way to provision & operate etcd at scale with close to zero manual intervention, while ensuring that your clusters are self-healing and resilient to failures? In this talk, we will introduce the open-source solutions that we built and maintain at Gardener that solves these challenges, and share lessons learned from 8+ years of operating production-grade etcd at scale and how we built etcd-druid and etcd-backup-restore to simplify spinning up and managing HA etcd clusters, reliably automate tasks like scheduled backups, corruption detection, quorum loss recovery, member replacement, scheduled defragmentation and providing a way to interact with the running clusters with out of band operations through opsTask and more, with zero recorded data loss across thousands of clusters.

Actively used in production by SAP through Gardener, Akamai Cloud, StackIT, and Scaleway, etcd-druid & etcd-backup-restore are now Linux Foundation projects under Linux Foundation EU via the NeoNephos Foundation, with an active maintainer community. We'll show what it takes to run etcd reliably at scale, and make it truly self-healing.
